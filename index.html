How we choose our picks

We do not claim to test every pot ourselves in a lab. Instead, we base our recommendations on years of owner feedback from cooking forums, long-term reviews from trusted culinary publications, and reports from home cooks who have used these pots for decades. We look for what actually breaks: chipped enamel, rusted rims, loose handles, and warped bottoms. A model that consistently earns praise for its durability and cooking performance over many years earns our pick. We also weigh factors like weight (a lighter pot is easier to handle but may not hold heat as well), lid fit (a heavy, tight lid means less moisture loss), and handle design (large enough for oven mitts). We prioritize pots that offer a clear advantage for their price, whether that means premium craftsmanship that justifies a higher cost or a budget-friendly option that punches far above its price tag. Every pick is a model we would confidently recommend to a friend.
